Noor - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Noor ("the app", "we") is an Islamic app that provides prayer times, Qibla direction, the Quran and daily Azkar. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum the app needs to work.

1. No account, no sign-in

Noor does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.

2. Location information

With your permission, the app uses your device's location (GPS or network location) for one purpose only: to calculate accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are.

  • Your location is processed on your device.
  • We do not send your location to our servers, and we do not operate any server that stores it. The app does not have a backend.
  • If you prefer not to share your location, you can deny the permission and choose a city manually inside the app.
  • You can withdraw the location permission at any time in your phone's settings.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves your choices locally on your phone, for example: selected language, chosen city, prayer calculation settings, notification preferences, Quran bookmark and reading settings. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules prayer time and Azkar reminders locally on your device. These notifications are not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app (Menu > Notifications) or in your phone's settings.

5. Advertising

Noor shows ads through Huawei Ads (HUAWEI Ads Kit, a third-party service). To deliver and measure ads, the Huawei Ads SDK may collect and process certain device and usage information, such as your advertising identifier (OAID), IP address, device model, operating system version, and ad interaction data. This is handled by Huawei under its own privacy policy:

Huawei Privacy Statement

You can limit ad personalization in your phone's settings (for example, the personalized ads / advertising ID options in Huawei's system settings).

6. Information we share

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with anyone, except that the Huawei Ads SDK processes the advertising-related data described above.

7. Internet use

Prayer times, Qibla and Quran content work without an internet connection. The app uses the internet only for showing ads and for opening links you choose (for example, rating or sharing the app).

8. Children

Noor is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Because your settings and location are processed and stored on your own device and not on our servers, we do not hold personal data that could be exposed in a server breach.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Deny or withdraw the location permission at any time.
  • Turn off notifications at any time.
  • Delete all locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
